Philosophy of Abu Bakr al-Razi

Persian Philosopher, Physician & Freethinker

Islamic Golden Age (864/865 - 925/935 CE)

Who Was Al-Razi?

  • Persian polymath of the Islamic Golden Age
  • Pioneering physician and medical theorist
  • Philosopher and skeptical thinker
  • Alchemist and empirical scientist

Life & Career

  • Born in Ray, near Tehran, Iran
  • Chief physician of Baghdad and Ray hospitals
  • Taught hundreds of students
  • Authored 200+ manuscripts

The Problem: Lost Works

  • Most philosophical works disappeared
  • Knowledge from hostile sources only
  • Fragmentary evidence survives
  • Scholarly interpretation remains contested

The Five Eternals

Core Metaphysical Doctrine

  • God
  • Soul (Psyche)
  • Matter
  • Time
  • Place

What They Mean

  • All five principles are eternal and uncreated
  • World emerges from their interaction
  • God is not sole creator
  • Departs from Islamic orthodoxy

Atomism

  • Matter consists of indivisible atoms
  • Atoms are eternal and unchanging
  • Rejects four-element theory
  • Ancient theory revived

Atomic Theory Impact

  • Explains properties not in classical theory
  • Oiliness, inflammability, salinity
  • Suggests deeper physical reality
  • Challenges established authorities

Rationalism

Reason as Supreme Authority

  • Reason is the tool for truth
  • God gave reason to all humans
  • Reason is sufficient for knowledge
  • No need for prophets

Universal Rationality

  • All humans have equal rational capacity
  • Differences from opportunity, not ability
  • Intellectual democracy principle
  • No natural hierarchy of minds

Discovering Truth

  • Reason alone discovers truth
  • No monopoly on knowledge
  • Future generations improve understanding
  • Continuous progress possible

Against Prophecy

  • Prophecy is unnecessary
  • Prophetic claims are delusional
  • Miracles prove nothing
  • Reason surpasses revelation

Religious Critique

  • All religions particularistic
  • Create unnecessary divisions
  • Claim false monopolies
  • Irrationality institutionalized

Against Religious Authority

  • No privileged access to truth
  • Scholars unjustly elevated
  • Hierarchies lack justification
  • All are equal reasoners

Taqlid: Blind Imitation

  • Reject unthinking acceptance
  • Question all authorities
  • Demand evidence and reason
  • Respect predecessors critically

Critical Methodology

  • Observation-based evidence
  • Compare claims to reality
  • Revise theories as needed
  • Rigorous documentation

Challenging Galen

  • Galen based on 3 cases
  • Al-Razi observed hundreds
  • Documented contradictions
  • Respectfully proposed corrections

Empirical Science

  • Pioneer of experimental method
  • Clinical observation primary
